INDOOR DAY

A family afternoon at the National Videogame Museum

A playful rainy-day plan that gives different generations something to share.

By Frisco Fieldnotes · Research-based guide · September 30, 2026

Choose an indoor anchor

The National Videogame Museum in Frisco explores gaming history through interactive exhibits. Giant Pong and the recreated 1980s bedroom offer different ways into the story. Before booking, use the museum’s own visitor information for current hours, admission, and any access questions.

Turn nostalgia into conversation

Ask each person to find a game, console, or exhibit they recognize. Then trade stories: what did you play, who did you play with, and what felt difficult? You do not need expert knowledge to enjoy the visit. Giving everyone a small discovery mission makes the outing more engaging than simply moving through the room.

Leave the rest of the day open

Avoid promising a specific visit duration; your group’s interest and the day’s crowd will shape the pace. Choose a meal after the museum rather than a second timed attraction. For younger visitors, agree on a break signal before you enter so the outing ends while everyone still has energy.
